Privacy

This research portfolio uses optional Google Analytics only after a visitor gives permission.

What is collected

When analytics is allowed, the site records a page view and an approximate city and country derived from the connection. The resulting statistics are available only to the site owner and are not displayed publicly.

No advertising

This portfolio does not display advertisements. Google Signals, advertising personalization, and enhanced interaction measurement are disabled.

What is not collected

This site does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, exact locations, or raw IP addresses in its analytics reports. Google Analytics uses an IP address to estimate location and discards it before the data is logged.

Your choice

Analytics is off by default. Your choice is saved in your browser and can be changed through “Privacy choices” in the portfolio footer.

Purpose and access

The data is used only to understand aggregate portfolio readership, such as how many countries are represented among visits. Analytics reports are private and are not sold or displayed publicly.
