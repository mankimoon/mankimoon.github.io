Thin-film interfaces 2026 · Ongoing

Metal/SWNT/PI Flexible Thin-Film Conductors

This project examines whether a buried SWNT mesh can serve as an engineered interphase beneath a metal thin film and help retain electrical continuity as cracks develop during tensile deformation.

Research question

How can interlayer topology and surface chemistry delay electrical failure in a metal film on a compliant PI substrate?

Work performed

Designed and fabricated the metal/SWNT/PI architecture, prepared nanotube interlayers under defined processing conditions, performed e-beam deposition, and developed a 3D-printed tensile-testing platform for synchronized resistance measurements.

Methods

E-beam evaporation, SWNT dispersion and filtration, electrical resistance-strain testing, SEM, Raman, FTIR, and quantitative crack analysis.

Current outcome

Preliminary full-cycle resistance data suggest that an intermediate SWNT loading provides the strongest retention of electrical continuity relative to the metal-only control. Current work is standardizing interlayer mass and thickness and using strain-synchronized resistance measurements and SEM crack analysis to verify the topology effect before isolating surface-chemistry contributions.

Interfacial transport 2025 · Ongoing

Spatial Raman Mapping, In Situ MoS₂ Vibrational Tracking, and Interfacial Contact Energetics in Electrospun PVA/PEDOT:PSS–GO/MoS₂ Thermoelectric Mats

This manuscript-stage study examines relationships between composition-dependent interfacial reorganization and thermoelectric transport in electrospun composite mats.

Research question

How do graphene oxide (GO) and MoS₂ alter the local conjugated environment, molecular contact, and coupled electrical-thermal transport of PEDOT:PSS-rich nanofibers?

Work performed

Integrated material fabrication, spatial and in situ spectroscopy, transport measurements, device assembly, quantitative analysis, and ORCA interaction-energy calculations.

Methods

Electrospinning, spatial Raman mapping, temperature-resolved Raman tracking, FTIR, thermoelectric characterization, Python analysis, and ORCA finite-cluster calculations.

Current outcome

Combined spatial spectroscopy, calculated interfacial energetics, and transport measurements, and assembled a five-unit serial thermoelectric device; manuscript in progress.

CNT thermal CVD Growth study · Modeling ongoing

Thermal CVD Growth and Reactive-Transport Modeling of CNTs

Hands-on vertical CNT-growth experiments using Al2O3/Fe-coated Cu films provide the experimental context for a compact OpenFOAM model of chamber-scale C2H2 transport, surface consumption, catalyst state, and CNT growth response.

Research question

How do substrate and catalyst conditions, gas transport, and surface consumption interact during CNT growth by thermal CVD?

Work performed

Deposited Al2O3 and Fe layers on Cu films, performed thermal CVD experiments for vertical CNT growth, examined cross-sectional morphology by SEM, and implemented a coupled transport-to-surface-response workflow.

Methods

Thermal CVD, cross-sectional SEM, OpenFOAM transport modeling, simplified reaction and catalyst-response terms, and Python-based post-processing.

Current outcome

Observed CNT growth on the Al2O3/Fe-coated Cu system by cross-sectional SEM and completed a baseline modeling and visualization workflow for future process-window studies.
