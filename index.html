I am an M.S. candidate in Materials Science and Engineering at Gachon University, expected to graduate in February 2027. Working in the Nano Synergy Laboratory under Professor Jae-Hee Han, I study thin-film growth and nanoscale interfaces in electronic and semiconductor materials, connecting surface chemistry and functional materials to device-relevant transport, integration, and reliability.

Research Projects

Thin-film interfaces 2026 · Ongoing

Al/CNT/PI Flexible Thin-Film Conductors

Buried SWNTs are studied as an engineered interphase beneath an aluminum film to preserve electrical continuity as cracks form during tensile deformation.

Research interest · CVD / ALD 2026 · Ongoing

Reactive-Transport Modeling for CNT CVD and CVD / ALD Thin-Film Growth

A compact OpenFOAM model connecting C₂H₂ transport, surface consumption, catalyst state, and CNT growth response. This forms my research interest in connecting precursor transport, thermal history, surface reaction, film morphology, and reliability for semiconductor and extreme-environment applications.

2025 · Ongoing Manuscript in progress

Spatial Raman Mapping, In Situ MoS₂ Vibrational Tracking, and Interfacial Contact Energetics in Electrospun PVA/PEDOT:PSS–GO/MoS₂ Thermoelectric Mats

Manuscript in progress. This study combines spatial Raman mapping, in situ MoS₂ vibrational tracking, FTIR, transport measurements, and DFT to establish how interfacial reorganization governs thermoelectric performance.
